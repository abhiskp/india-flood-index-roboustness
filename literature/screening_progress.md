## Current Novelty Decision

The literature gap can be claimed only narrowly. The defensible novelty is not external validation in general, but the combined India district DFSI setting, independent satellite comparison, strict non-overlapping future-fatality validation, simple historical baselines and state-block uncertainty analysis.
