# Screening Criteria

## Include

- Peer-reviewed flood-risk, vulnerability, severity or priority studies.
- Regional, district, city, catchment or similar geographic rankings.
- Papers that build, validate or compare composite indices.
- Temporal prediction of later flood impacts.
- Satellite-based validation.
- Spatial or geographic holdout validation.
- Methodological papers on composite indicators and ranking validation.
- Major systematic reviews.
- Important Indian flood-index studies.
- Papers mainly from 2015 onward, while retaining key earlier methodological work.

## Exclude

- Purely hydraulic forecasting with no prioritisation or ranking relevance.
- Building-level damage estimation without regional ranking.
- Papers with no accessible methodological detail.
- Unverified conference slides, blogs or generated citation lists.
- Duplicate preprint and journal versions.
- Studies unrelated to floods unless the validation method is directly transferable.

## Screening Notes

- Add a paper to `literature_matrix.csv` only after verifying bibliographic details against a publisher page, DOI record, PubMed, DOAJ, Crossref/OpenAlex, or another authoritative database.
- For preprints and conference abstracts, prefer the peer-reviewed journal version when available.
- Do not treat citation counts, AI summaries or unsourced blog posts as verification.
- Separate validation of physical hazard, exposure, vulnerability, severity and future human consequences where the paper's construct allows it.
